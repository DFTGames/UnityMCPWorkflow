# Levels

%% Give each level its own page once designed in detail. %%

The campaign has **8 levels**, each about 3 to 4 minutes of waves ending with a boss. Endless mode reuses the settings and bosses ([[Core Loop#Endless mode]]).

| # | Setting | Boss | Boss signature |
| - | ------- | ---- | -------------- |
| 1 | Magenta Nebula | Hive Carrier | Launches Darts; exposed weak core. |
| 2 | Asteroid Belt | Rock Crusher | Hurls meteors. |
| 3 | Solar Corona | Sunforge | Sweeping flame beams. |
| 4 | Ice Rings | Frost Lancer | Fast dashes across the screen. |
| 5 | Derelict Fleet | Dreadnought | Multiple turrets destroyed one by one. |
| 6 | Ion Storm | Tempest | Lightning arcs that make parts of the screen dangerous. |
| 7 | Black Hole's Edge | Singularity Engine | Gravity pull on the player. |
| 8 | Enemy Homeworld | The Overmind | Multi-phase fight remixing earlier bosses' attacks. |

New enemy types are introduced per the "First level" column in [[Enemies and Hazards]].
