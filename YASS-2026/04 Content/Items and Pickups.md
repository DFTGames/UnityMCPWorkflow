# Items and Pickups

## Pickups
| Name | Effect | Drop weight |
| ---- | ------ | ----------- |
| Health | Restores health (amount to be tuned) | 40 |
| Weapon upgrade | Raises the weapon one level; 500 points at level 5 ([[Mechanics#Weapon upgrades]]) | 35 |
| Shield | 3 hits or 10 seconds ([[Mechanics#Shield]]) | 20 |
| Extra life | Grants one life | 5 |

## Drop rules
| Source | Drop chance |
| ------ | ----------- |
| Enemy (non-boss) | 8% |
| Solid meteor | 15% |
| Splitting meteor (any size) | 0% (prevents farming fragments) |
| Boss | Always: 1 weapon upgrade and 1 health |

- Drop chance is scaled by the difficulty's pickup drop multiplier ([[Difficulty and Balancing]]).
- When a drop happens, the pickup type is chosen by the weights above.
- **Pity rule:** if the weapon is below level 3 and no weapon upgrade has been collected for 45 seconds, the next drop is a weapon upgrade.
- Pickups drift left with the scroll and despawn when they leave the screen.
