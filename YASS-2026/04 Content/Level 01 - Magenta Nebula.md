# Level 01 - Magenta Nebula

Setting: magenta and purple nebula (see [[Art Direction]]). Length: about 3.5 minutes. Enemies: Dart, Weaver; splitting and solid meteors. Boss: [[Hive Carrier]].

## Waves
Counts are for Pilot; the enemy count multiplier scales each group ([[Wave System]]). Meteor fields use the Scatter formation, one group per meteor type. The final rush's Darts are two groups of 5, so on Ace they scale to 7 + 7.

| # | Wave | Groups |
| - | ---- | ------ |
| 1 | Introduction | 5 Darts, line, centre |
| 2 | Weavers | 3 Weavers, staggered |
| 3 | Meteor field | 4 large splitting meteors, spread across the height |
| 4 | Dart V | 7 Darts, V formation, centre |
| 5 | Mixed | 4 Weavers + 4 Darts |
| 6 | Meteor field | 3 large splitting + 2 solid meteors |
| 7 | Pincer | Two columns of 4 Darts, top and bottom |
| 8 | Weaver screen | 6 Weavers, staggered |
| 9 | Pressure | 6 Darts + 3 Weavers + 2 meteors |
| 10 | Meteor storm | 8 mixed meteors |
| 11 | Final rush | 10 Darts + 4 Weavers |
| Boss | Hive Carrier | See [[Hive Carrier]] |
