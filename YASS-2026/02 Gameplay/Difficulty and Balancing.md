# Difficulty and Balancing

## Difficulty levels
Three difficulties, as in the original (names to be confirmed). Harder settings change:
- **Enemies:** more enemies, faster enemies and bullets.
- **Player toughness:** fewer lives and/or less health.
- **Score:** a higher score multiplier.

Leaderboards are kept separately per difficulty and mode.

## Difficulty curve
Waves escalate within a level and across the 8 levels. The game should stay hard: the original was known for most players not surviving.
