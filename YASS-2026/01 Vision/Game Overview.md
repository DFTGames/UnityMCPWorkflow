# Game Overview

%% The one-page summary of the game. Everything else in the GDD should serve what is written here. %%

## High concept
A faithful 2026 remake of **YASS** (DFT Games Studios, Xbox 360, 2010): a fast, hard, side-scrolling space shooter where you blast through waves of enemies, bosses and meteors.

## Elevator pitch
YASS 2026 brings the 2010 Xbox Live Indie Games shooter to PC, mobile and the browser. Fly through eight hostile sectors, each guarded by a boss, grab weapon upgrades and shields, and chase the top of the global leaderboards on three difficulty levels. Survive the campaign, then see how long you last in Endless mode.

## Design pillars
1. **Faithful to the original.** Same structure, feel and identity as the 2010 game; modernise, do not reinvent.
2. **Fast and tough.** Constant pressure; the original was known for most players not surviving.
3. **Score chasing.** Every run feeds a cross-platform leaderboard, per difficulty and mode.
4. **Play anywhere.** Equally good with gamepad, mouse and keyboard, or touch.
5. **Built for co-op.** Single player first, but architected so up to 4-player online co-op (Photon Fusion) can be added without a rewrite.

## Genre
2D side-scrolling shoot-em-up with twin-stick controls.

## Target audience
Arcade and shmup players, fans of the original, and players who like short, replayable high-score runs.

## Platforms
- PC (Steam, Windows)
- Mobile (iOS and Android)
- Web (WebGL, for example itch.io)

## Business model
Free, non-commercial: a tutorial and portfolio project. No ads or in-app purchases.

## The 2010 original (reference)
- Released 22 April 2010 on Xbox Live Indie Games; delisted after the publisher closed.
- "A fast side-scrolling space shooter, playable in co-op for up to four players, with live high scores and three difficulty levels."
- Player on the left, enemy waves from the right; painted magenta and purple nebula backgrounds; per-player HUD with heart lives, health bar, kill count and gamertag.
- Title menu: Play, Quit, Credits, Tell a Friend.
- Sources: [DFT Games: YASS](https://dftgames.com/yass/), [YASS for Xbox 360 (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LmVWfxo9iz0), [YASS Multiplayer promo (video)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lngaldtwWzQ).
- No original source code, design documents or assets survive; details come from the videos and the original designer's memory.
